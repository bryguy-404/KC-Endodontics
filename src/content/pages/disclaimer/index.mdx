## Notice to website visitors

This website is provided for general information and education. Using the website does not establish a doctor-patient relationship with KC Endodontics or Dr. J. Eric Moore. The website does not provide a diagnosis or an individualized treatment plan and is not a substitute for an examination or advice from a qualified dental or medical professional.

No guarantee or warranty is made about how general information on this website applies to a particular person, tooth, diagnosis, or outcome. Treatment decisions should be made with an appropriate clinician who can review the complete circumstances.

If you believe you have a dental emergency, call KC Endodontics at [913-642-3636](tel:+19136423636). For a life-threatening medical emergency, call 911.

## Privacy and personal information

The current website is phone-first and does not include an online appointment-request form or a form that transmits patient records. Information entered into the printable referral worksheet remains in the visitor’s browser unless the visitor chooses to print it; the website does not submit or store those worksheet entries.

Do not send protected health information through ordinary, unsecured email. Dental offices should call KC Endodontics to confirm the practice’s current secure method for sending referrals, diagnostic images, or clinical records.

Standard hosting systems may process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as an internet protocol address, browser type, requested page, referring page, and date or time of access. This information may be used for security, reliability, and aggregate site administration.

Information shared by telephone, fax, or during clinical care is handled through the practice’s separate office and patient-privacy procedures.

## External services and links

This website links to outside organizations and services, including Google Maps and professional health-information resources. Those websites control their own content, availability, accessibility, cookies, and privacy practices. A link is provided for convenience and does not imply that KC Endodontics controls or endorses everything published by the outside site.

## Accessibility

KC Endodontics strives to make this website usable for people with disabilities and continues to improve content and navigation. If any part of the website does not meet your needs, call [913-642-3636](tel:+19136423636) for assistance or an alternative way to receive the information.
