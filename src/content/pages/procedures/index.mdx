## Care designed around the tooth in front of us

Endodontics is the dental specialty focused on diagnosing tooth pain and treating the tissue inside a tooth. At KC Endodontics, each recommendation begins with a careful examination, imaging, and a clear discussion of the available options.

Treatment may be straightforward, or it may involve a previously treated tooth, a crack that is difficult to locate, or an injury that requires timely attention. The goal is always the same: address the source of the problem and preserve your natural tooth when it can be predictably saved.

## What to expect from a specialist evaluation

- A focused review of your symptoms and dental history
- Diagnostic testing and imaging appropriate to the tooth
- A plain-language explanation of the findings
- A discussion of benefits, limitations, alternatives, and next steps
- Coordination with your general or restorative dentist when follow-up care is needed

> Not every tooth needs the same treatment. A consultation helps determine whether endodontic care is appropriate and which option best fits your situation.
