  <h2 id="anesthesiologist-title">Administered and monitored by a board-eligible anesthesiologist.</h2>
  <p>For patients who want deeper comfort, KC Endodontics offers IV sedation administered and monitored by a board-eligible anesthesiologist.   <h2 id="sedation-nitrous-title">Nitrous oxide for children and adults.</h2>
  <p>Nitrous oxide, often called laughing gas, is available when appropriate and is provided by Dr. Moore during your visit. It is breathed through a small mask and is intended to help you feel calmer while remaining awake and able to communicate with the team.</p>
  <p>Nitrous oxide does not require an insurance eligibility check or authorization and is paid for out of pocket at the time of your visit. Every patient is different, and Dr. Moore will discuss whether nitrous oxide fits your appointment.</p>

<section className="sedation-section" aria-labelledby="sedation-comfort-title">
  <p className="eyebrow text-secondary">Who sedation may help</p>
  <h2 id="sedation-comfort-title">Comfort support for more than dental anxiety.</h2>
  <p>A sedation conversation may be useful when fear, sensitivity, or the length of treatment makes it harder to receive the care you need.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>You feel nervous, anxious, or fearful about dental treatment</li>
    <li>You have a sensitive gag reflex</li>
    <li>You have difficulty becoming numb with local anesthetic alone</li>
    <li>You find it hard to sit still through a longer appointment</li>
    <li>You need several procedures and want to discuss a more comfortable plan</li>
    <li>You have delayed dental care because of fear or a previous experience</li>
  </ul>
</section>
