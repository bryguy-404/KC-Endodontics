## Patient education and treatment safety

- [AAE Root Canal Safety](https://www.aae.org/specialty/clinical-resources/root-canal-safety/) — Evidence, patient resources, and professional materials addressing endodontic safety and misinformation.
- [AAE Myths About Root Canals](https://www.aae.org/patients/root-canal-treatment/myths-root-canals/) — Patient-focused answers to common misconceptions about treatment.
- [KC Endodontics Tooth-Saving Tips](/patient-information/tooth-saving-tips/) — Practical guidance about preserving natural teeth and seeking timely evaluation.

## Clinical assessment and treatment planning

- [AAE Case Assessment Tools](https://www.aae.org/specialty/clinical-resources/treatment-planning/case-assessment-tools/) — Case-difficulty forms, references, referral guidance, and the EndoCase application.
- [AAE Treatment Options Guide](https://www.aae.org/specialty/clinical-resources/treatment-planning/treatment-options-guide/) — Clinical considerations for compromised natural teeth and reasonable treatment alternatives.
- [AAE Traumatic Dental Injuries](https://www.aae.org/specialty/clinical-resources/treatment-planning/traumatic-dental-injuries/) — Guidelines and patient resources for dental trauma.
- [AAE Glossary of Endodontic Terms](https://www.aae.org/specialty/clinical-resources/glossary-endodontic-terms/) — Terminology for endodontic diagnosis and care.

## Newsletters and continuing education

- [ENDODONTICS: Colleagues for Excellence](https://www.aae.org/specialty/publications-research/endodontics-colleagues-excellence/) — Clinical newsletters and the AAE’s historical archive.
- [Colleagues for Excellence on Endo On Demand](https://endoondemand.aae.org/colleagues) — Continuing-education access managed by the AAE.

## Using these resources

For archived publications, check the publication date and any updates or cautions provided by the AAE.

These references provide general information and do not replace diagnosis, clinical judgment, informed consent, or consultation about a particular patient.
