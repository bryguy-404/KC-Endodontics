## Call the Prairie Village office

The fastest way to schedule or get help with a time-sensitive dental concern is to call the team directly.

**Phone:** [913-642-3636](tel:+19136423636)  
**Fax:** 913-642-5066

If you reach the office outside posted hours, follow the voicemail instructions. For a life-threatening medical emergency, call 911.

## Posted office hours

- **Monday:** 8am–4pm
- **Wednesday–Thursday:** 8am–4pm

Availability can change for holidays, emergencies, or clinical scheduling. Please call before visiting if you do not already have a confirmed appointment.

## When you call

It helps to have the following available:

- Your contact information
- The tooth or area involved
- A brief description of symptoms and when they began
- Whether swelling, fever, or a dental injury is involved
- Your referring dentist’s name, when applicable
- Your dental insurance information

The team will help determine whether you need an evaluation, what records may be useful, and how soon the office should see you.
