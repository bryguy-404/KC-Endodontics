## Instructions support your individual care plan

The details of your appointment may change what you should eat, which medications you should take, whether you need transportation, and how you care for the tooth afterward. The instructions provided directly by KC Endodontics take priority over general website information.

Use the three sections below to prepare, care for the treatment area, and understand the restorative steps that follow endodontic treatment.

## When to call

Call **913-642-3636** whenever an instruction is unclear or when symptoms are severe, increasing, or different from what the team asked you to expect. Rapidly increasing swelling or difficulty breathing or swallowing requires emergency medical attention.

> Do not start, stop, or change a prescribed medication unless the prescribing clinician or KC Endodontics has given you specific guidance.
