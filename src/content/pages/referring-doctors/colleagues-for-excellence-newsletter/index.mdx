## A current clinical library for general dentists

*ENDODONTICS: Colleagues for Excellence* is a clinical newsletter series from the American Association of Endodontists. It is designed to support practicing general dentists with focused updates that can inform diagnosis, treatment planning, patient conversations, and referral decisions.

[Browse the current AAE newsletter library](https://www.aae.org/specialty/publications-research/endodontics-colleagues-excellence/)

## Recent and frequently referenced topics

The maintained AAE library includes current and archived issues covering topics such as:

- Regenerative endodontics
- Root resorption
- Cracked teeth and vertical root fractures
- Endodontics and neurovascular injury
- The endodontic-restorative interface
- Antibiotic use in endodontics
- Maxillary sinusitis of endodontic origin
- Cone beam computed tomography
- Endodontic emergencies
- Nonsurgical retreatment
- Endodontic microsurgery
- Case difficulty assessment and referral

Because clinical guidance and linked documents change over time, the AAE library should be treated as the current source rather than a copied list of older PDF files.

## Continuing education

Some newsletter topics are also available through the AAE’s Endo On Demand platform with continuing-education options. Access requirements and availability are managed by the AAE.

[View Colleagues for Excellence on Endo On Demand](https://endoondemand.aae.org/colleagues)
