## Endodontic and dental organizations

- [American Association of Endodontists](https://www.aae.org/) — Professional and patient resources focused on endodontics and saving natural teeth.
- [American Dental Association](https://www.ada.org/) — Professional standards, policy, scientific resources, and practice guidance.
- [MouthHealthy](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/) — Consumer oral-health information from the American Dental Association.
- [American Association of Women Dentists](https://www.aawd.org/) — Professional organization supporting the participation and leadership of women in dentistry.

## Broader health information

- [American Medical Association](https://www.ama-assn.org/) — Professional medical information, policy, and public-health resources.
- [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/) — Patient-friendly health information from the U.S. National Library of Medicine.
- [CDC Oral Health](https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/) — Public-health information and data related to oral health.

## A note about outside websites

External websites manage their own content, privacy practices, and accessibility. Links are provided for convenience and do not replace individualized dental or medical advice.
