## Download the printable form

[Open the current KC Endodontics referral form (PDF)](https://www.kcendodontics.com/files/2020/02/PV-referral-form.pdf)

The current form is a two-page printable document. Complete the relevant patient, referring-office, tooth, diagnostic, and treatment-request information before sending it with applicable images or records.

## Protect patient information

Do not transmit protected patient information through ordinary, unsecured email or an unapproved web form. Before sending records electronically, call KC Endodontics to confirm the practice’s current secure process.

## Helpful referral details

Including the following can reduce back-and-forth and help the patient’s first visit proceed smoothly:

- The tooth or area of concern
- The reason for the referral
- Relevant treatment history
- Recent images
- Restorative plans or limitations
- Preferred treatment scope
- Best contact information for follow-up
