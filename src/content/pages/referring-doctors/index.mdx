## Thank you for trusting us with your patients

KC Endodontics values the confidence referring doctors place in the practice. Dr. Moore and the team work to make the referral experience straightforward for the patient while keeping the restorative dentist informed.

## What to send with a referral

When available, please include:

- The tooth or area to be evaluated
- The reason for referral and relevant symptoms
- Recent diagnostic images
- Pertinent treatment history and restorative considerations
- Current medications or medical considerations that may affect care
- Your preferred scope of treatment and follow-up communication

Please do not send private patient information through ordinary, unsecured email. Call the office at [913-642-3636](tel:+19136423636) if you need help choosing a secure delivery method.

## Communication and restorative follow-up

Diagnostic findings and completed care are communicated to the referring office. When a final restoration or other follow-up is needed, the patient is directed back to the restorative dentist with clear timing guidance.

For an urgent referral or a case you would like to discuss directly, call the office. The team will help coordinate the most appropriate next step.
