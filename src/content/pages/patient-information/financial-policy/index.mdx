## Patient billing

Payment is generally due when service is provided unless another arrangement has been approved in advance. KC Endodontics currently lists Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, cash, check, and CareCredit among its payment options. Please call to confirm the payment methods available for your appointment. All patients are welcome to ask about sedation. Missouri Medicaid is accepted for [sedation](/procedures/sedation-dentistry/) cases, and nitrous oxide is paid for at the time of your visit.

If you have questions about a treatment estimate or account balance, call **913-642-3636**. A conversation with the team can often resolve uncertainty before the day of care.

## Insurance and patient responsibility

Insurance coverage is an agreement between you and your insurer. The office may provide estimates and submit claims as a courtesy, but the patient or responsible party remains responsible for fees not paid by the plan.

Benefits, deductibles, annual limits, and plan participation can change. Any estimate is based on the information available at that time and is not a guarantee of payment by the insurance company.

## Arrangements before treatment

If you need to discuss payment arrangements or third-party financing, please do so before the procedure whenever possible. The team can explain payment options and help you identify the information needed to make a decision.
