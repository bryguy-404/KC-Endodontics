## Office hours

- **Monday:** 8am–4pm
- **Wednesday–Thursday:** 8am–4pm

Office hours and availability may change. Please call to confirm timing and schedule your visit.

## When you call

Be prepared to share your contact information, the tooth or area involved, how long symptoms have been present, whether swelling or injury is involved, and the name of your referring or restorative dentist when applicable.

KC Endodontics schedules appointments as promptly as possible and makes every effort to prioritize urgent pain and dental injuries. Endodontic procedures and emergencies can sometimes affect the day’s schedule; the team appreciates your patience and will communicate if timing changes.

## Schedule by phone

Call **913-642-3636** with questions or to request an appointment. For privacy, do not send sensitive health information through an ordinary website message or social-media channel.

> An online request would not confirm an appointment. A direct phone conversation remains the fastest way to explain your needs and confirm scheduling.
