Insurance participation and network status can change. Please call the office with your exact plan information before treatment so the team can confirm the current relationship and explain what can be estimated.

## Claims and estimates

As a courtesy, the office can submit claims and track their progress. Keep KC Endodontics informed of plan changes and provide accurate subscriber information. Your insurer determines the final benefit, and you are responsible for charges not paid by the plan.

## Payment options

The office currently accepts cash, check, major credit cards, and CareCredit. Payment is generally due at the time of service unless another arrangement has been approved in advance.

> “Covered” does not always mean paid in full. Deductibles, annual maximums, waiting periods, exclusions, and plan limitations may affect your final responsibility.
