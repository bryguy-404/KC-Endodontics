## Welcome to KC Endodontics

KC Endodontics is dedicated exclusively to endodontic care. Dr. Moore and the team provide professional specialty treatment in a friendly, comfortable environment, with time to explain what has been found and what comes next.

These pages cover the practical and clinical questions patients most often have before calling, before arriving, and after treatment. If your question is specific to your tooth, medical history, medication, insurance, or appointment, please call the office so the team can give you guidance that fits your situation.

## A simple path through the information

- **Preparing for care:** First Visit, Scheduling, and Instructions
- **Understanding costs:** Financial Policy, Insurance Information, and CareCredit
- **Understanding your tooth:** About Your Tooth, Tooth Pain, and Tooth-Saving Tips
- **Understanding the practice:** Why Choose an Endodontist, Advanced Technology, Endodontic FAQ, and Nitrous Oxide

> Website information can help you prepare, but it cannot replace an examination or the instructions provided for your individual care.
