## Financing that is separate from the practice

CareCredit is a healthcare credit card offered and administered by a third party. Approved applicants may use it for eligible out-of-pocket dental expenses, depending on the provider’s terms and the transaction. All patients are welcome to ask about sedation. Missouri Medicaid is accepted for [sedation](/procedures/sedation-dentistry/) cases, and nitrous oxide is paid for at the time of your visit.

KC Endodontics does not set CareCredit’s interest rates, promotional periods, eligibility rules, or credit decisions. Those details can change, so review the complete agreement before applying or using the account.

## Review financing terms

Visit [CareCredit’s official website](https://www.carecredit.com/) to review financing information, check eligibility, and understand the terms that would apply to you. Applying may involve a credit decision; follow the provider’s disclosures carefully.

- [See if you prequalify or apply with CareCredit](https://www.carecredit.com/apply/)
- [Estimate payments with CareCredit’s calculator](https://www.carecredit.com/providercenter/resourcescenter/payment_calculator/)

The calculator provides an estimate only. Available promotions and the terms that apply are determined by CareCredit and may vary by provider and transaction.

You can also call KC Endodontics at **913-642-3636** to ask whether CareCredit can be used for the care being discussed and what portion is expected to be due.

> Financing can change when payment is made, but it does not change the total clinical recommendation. Make sure both the treatment plan and the financial terms are clear before proceeding.
