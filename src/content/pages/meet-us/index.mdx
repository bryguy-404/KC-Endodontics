## Specialty care centered on the person

KC Endodontics is a practice dedicated exclusively to endodontic care. The team’s role is not only to evaluate and treat the inside of the tooth, but also to make a potentially stressful visit feel organized, understandable, and respectful.

Patients are given time to describe their symptoms, review diagnostic findings, and ask questions. When treatment is recommended, Dr. Moore explains the goal, reasonable alternatives, and the restorative follow-up that may be needed with the general dentist.

## Meet J. Eric Moore, DDS

Dr. Moore completed advanced specialty training in endodontics after graduating with honors from dental school. His approach reflects the qualities emphasized throughout the current practice website: professional ethics, compassion for patients, and careful endodontic technique.

## Working with your general dentist

Endodontic care is often one part of a larger restorative plan. KC Endodontics communicates with referring dentists so diagnostic findings, treatment, and the next restorative step remain coordinated.

If you have a referral, bring it with you or ask your dentist to forward relevant records. A referral is not required to call the office with questions about scheduling an evaluation.
