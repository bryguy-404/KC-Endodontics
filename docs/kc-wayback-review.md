## Scope and limitations

Identified and attempted 39 distinct KC page URLs. Recovered practice-page content from 29; 10 remained unavailable. Capture dates for readable pages range from February 2024 to June 2025. Crawl inventory and image results are recorded below. Same-site navigation and body links were followed; duplicate trailing-slash routes were consolidated. Malformed Facebook links were recognized as broken external references rather than additional practice pages. External clinical bibliographies and provider websites were outside this review of KC business details.

Some archive pages and most attempted photo files timed out. Image URLs and background-image attributes were still inspected in successfully recovered HTML. One recovered PBHS photo was visually inspected and shows generic patient imagery. No conclusion here depends on treating a failed download as proof that the original asset never existed.

No website copy, operational claims, forms, tracking or publication settings were changed during this archive review. The previously completed implementation list remains in `kc-september-18-review.md`; this document supersedes its broad question list with the more specific questions above.
